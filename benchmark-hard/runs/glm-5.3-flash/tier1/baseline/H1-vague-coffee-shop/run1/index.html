Our story

About

Ember & Oak opened in 2019 in a former hardware store. We roast in small batches every Tuesday. Everything in the case is baked before sunrise.

“Come as a customer twice and we’ll call it your regular order.”

— Mara & Dev, owners
  • Small-batch roasted in house
  • Milk from Alder Creek Dairy
  • Pastries baked each morning
  • Compostable cups & lids
