Good to know

Where do I park?

There is a free lot behind the shop — turn onto Kiln Alley and it’s on your right. Street parking on Lantern Lane is metered until 6 pm.

Can I bring my dog?

On the patio, absolutely — water bowls included. Inside is service animals only, per the health department. Milo will be polite about it.

Wi-Fi and laptops?

Yes — network “CopperCupGuest”, password on the receipt. Laptops are welcome all day on weekdays and until noon on weekends, so the corner booth stays a conversation spot on Saturdays.
