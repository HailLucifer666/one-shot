Our story

A café the size of a living room

We opened in 2016 with a one-group machine, a single grinder, and a theory: if the coffee is honest and the room is warm, people come back. The theory held.

These days we roast our own beans two blocks away, bake before sunrise, and know most orders by heart. But the booth is still first-come, the Wi-Fi password is still on the receipt, and the door is still propped open on the first warm day of spring.
