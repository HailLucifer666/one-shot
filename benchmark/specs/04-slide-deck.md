# Spec 04: Slide deck

Create an 8-slide deck, as an HTML slide page or a .pptx file, titled Why Small Teams Should Write Things Down. Audience: managers at companies of 10 to 50 people.

## Requirements
- Slides: title, problem, cost of not writing, three practices (one slide each), a 30-day starter plan, closing call to action.
- One message per slide, a headline stating the takeaway, at most 40 words per slide.
- Speaker notes on every slide.

## Acceptance criteria (yes or no)
1. Exactly 8 slides in the stated order.
2. Every slide has a takeaway headline, not a topic label.
3. No slide exceeds 40 words, excluding speaker notes.
4. Speaker notes exist on all 8 slides.
5. Consistent fonts, colors and layout across slides.
6. Text is at least 24 pt equivalent and has at least 4.5:1 contrast.
7. No invented statistics presented as fact.
8. Navigation works (arrow keys for HTML, or opens in PowerPoint without errors).
9. The closing slide has one clear action.
10. File opens without repair prompts or console errors.
